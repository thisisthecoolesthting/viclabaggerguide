## Top 5 Harley Bagger Infotainment System Upgrades  

Harley-Davidson baggers are built for long-distance comfort and style, but the factory infotainment systems—while functional—can sometimes leave riders wanting more. Whether you're looking for better navigation, crisper audio, or seamless smartphone integration, upgrading your infotainment system can transform your ride. Here are the top five upgrades to consider for your Harley bagger.  

### 1. **Boom!™ GTS 6.5" Infotainment System**  

Harley-Davidson’s **Boom!™ GTS** is the most direct upgrade for riders who want a factory-like experience with enhanced features. This 6.5" touchscreen system is designed specifically for touring models and offers:  

- **High-resolution display** with glove-friendly responsiveness  
- **Built-in navigation** powered by HERE Maps, including real-time traffic updates  
- **Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™** for seamless smartphone mirroring  
- **Bluetooth 5.0** for stable wireless audio streaming  
- **Customizable ride data** (fuel range, tire pressure, etc.)  

**Best for:** Riders who want a plug-and-play OEM upgrade with full Harley-Davidson integration.  

### 2. **Rockford Fosgate Harley-Davidson Audio Systems**  

If your priority is **premium sound**, Rockford Fosgate’s Harley-specific audio upgrades are a game-changer. Their **Stage II and Stage III speaker and amplifier kits** are engineered for baggers, delivering:  

- **Punch® 6.5" speakers** with high-efficiency tweeters for crisp highs  
- **Powerful amplifiers** (up to 400W) to cut through wind and road noise  
- **Plug-and-play harnesses** for easy installation on Street Glide, Road Glide, and Electra Glide models  
- **Tuned enclosures** to maximize bass response without distortion  

**Best for:** Audiophiles who demand concert-quality sound on the road.  

### 3. **Garmin Tread® Overland GPS for Motorcycles**  

For adventure-focused bagger riders, the **Garmin Tread® Overland** is a rugged, motorcycle-specific GPS that complements or replaces factory navigation. Key features include:  

- **5.5" glove-compatible touchscreen** with off-road trail mapping  
- **Group Ride Radio** for real-time communication with other riders  
- **Preloaded topographic maps** and public land boundaries  
- **Weather and hazard alerts** via the Garmin Drive™ app  
- **Mounting kits** available for Harley batwing and sharknose fairings  

**Best for:** Touring riders who explore both highways and backroads.  

### 4. **Plugfones Liberate 2.0 Bluetooth Earplugs**  

Sometimes the best infotainment upgrade isn’t a screen—it’s **hearing protection with built-in audio**. The **Plugfones Liberate 2.0** are OSHA-rated earplugs with:  

- **Bluetooth 5.0** for wireless music, calls, and GPS prompts  
- **Noise Reduction Rating (NRR) of 27dB** to protect against wind noise  
- **10-hour battery life** with quick charging  
- **Sweatproof design** for all-day comfort  

**Best for:** Riders who want hearing protection without sacrificing audio quality.  

### 5. **Wireless Charging & USB Hub Upgrades**  

Modern infotainment relies on keeping devices powered. Upgrading to a **wireless charging pad** (like Harley’s **Factory Wireless Charger**) or a **high-output USB hub** (such as the **Eastern Beaver PC-8**) ensures your phone and accessories stay charged. Look for:  

- **Qi-compatible charging** (for iPhones and Android devices)  
- **Weatherproof USB-C ports** with fast-charging support  
- **Clean, integrated mounting** (e.g., dash or fairing pockets)  

**Best for:** Tech-savvy riders who rely on smartphones for navigation and music.  

### **What to Look for in a Bagger Infotainment Upgrade**  

When choosing an upgrade, consider:  

- **Compatibility** – Ensure the system works with your Harley model (e.g., M8 vs. Twin Cam).  
- **Ease of Installation** – Plug-and-play systems save time vs. custom wiring.  
- **Audio Quality** – Look for high-wattage amps and marine-grade speakers if sound is a priority.  
- **Smartphone Integration** – Apple CarPlay®/Android Auto™ reduce reliance on built-in nav.  
- **Durability** – Waterproof and vibration-resistant components last longer.  

### **Final Thoughts**  

Whether you’re chasing better sound, smarter navigation, or just a more connected ride, these upgrades can elevate your Harley bagger’s infotainment experience. Stick with trusted brands and Harley-specific kits for seamless integration—your next long ride will thank you.
