# Buying Guide: Harley-Davidson Oil Filter Options  

Keeping your Harley-Davidson bagger or touring motorcycle running smoothly requires regular oil changes, and choosing the right oil filter is just as important as selecting the right oil. Harley-Davidson engines demand high-quality filtration to protect against contaminants and ensure optimal performance. This guide covers the key oil filter options available, their features, and what to look for when making your selection.  

## Types of Harley-Davidson Oil Filters  

Harley-Davidson motorcycles typically use spin-on oil filters, which are easy to install and replace. There are two main types:  

1. **Standard Paper Media Filters** – These are the most common and cost-effective options. They use cellulose or synthetic fiber media to trap contaminants. Harley-Davidson’s **Genuine Oil Filter (p/n 63804-99B)** is a popular choice, designed specifically for Twin Cam, Milwaukee-Eight, and Evolution engines.  

2. **High-Performance Synthetic Media Filters** – These filters use advanced synthetic materials for finer filtration and longer service life. The **Harley-Davidson Screamin’ Eagle High-Performance Oil Filter (p/n 63798-99B)** is an example, offering superior dirt-holding capacity and better flow under extreme conditions.  

## Key Features to Consider  

### **Filtration Efficiency**  
A good oil filter should capture small particles (typically 20-40 microns) without restricting oil flow. High-performance synthetic filters often provide better efficiency than standard paper filters.  

### **Anti-Drainback Valve**  
This feature prevents oil from draining out of the filter when the engine is off, ensuring immediate lubrication at startup. Most Harley-Davidson OEM filters include this.  

### **Bypass Valve**  
If the filter becomes clogged, the bypass valve allows oil to continue circulating (unfiltered) to prevent engine damage. A well-designed bypass system is critical for engine protection.  

### **Thread Size & Compatibility**  
Ensure the filter matches your bike’s specifications. Most modern Harley-Davidson models (Twin Cam, Milwaukee-Eight) use a **20mm x 1.5 thread pitch**, but older models may differ.  

### **Construction Quality**  
Look for a sturdy metal casing and a durable gasket to prevent leaks. OEM Harley-Davidson filters are built to withstand vibration and heat common in big-twin engines.  

## Harley-Davidson OEM vs. Aftermarket Filters  

While Harley-Davidson’s **Genuine Oil Filters** are engineered specifically for their engines, some riders opt for aftermarket alternatives. Here’s how they compare:  

- **OEM Filters** – Designed to exact Harley-Davidson specifications, ensuring proper fit and filtration. They often include all necessary valves and meet factory performance standards.  
- **Aftermarket Filters** – Some brands offer comparable or even enhanced filtration, but quality varies. Stick to reputable manufacturers that meet or exceed OEM specs.  

## What to Look for in a Harley-Davidson Oil Filter  

When shopping for an oil filter, keep these factors in mind:  

- **Compatibility** – Always check your owner’s manual or Harley-Davidson’s parts catalog for the correct filter for your model and engine.  
- **Filtration Rating** – A higher efficiency rating (measured in microns) means better protection.  
- **Durability** – Look for robust construction, especially if you ride in harsh conditions.  
- **Service Life** – Some synthetic filters can last longer between changes, but always follow Harley-Davidson’s recommended oil change intervals.  

## Recommended Oil Filters for Popular Models  

- **Milwaukee-Eight Engines (2017+)** – **Harley-Davidson Genuine Oil Filter (p/n 63804-99B)**  
- **Twin Cam Engines (1999-2017)** – **Screamin’ Eagle High-Performance Oil Filter (p/n 63798-99B)**  
- **Evolution Engines (1984-1999)** – **Harley-Davidson Genuine Oil Filter (p/n 63794-99A)**  

## Final Tips  

- Always replace the filter with every oil change.  
- Use the correct oil type and capacity as specified in your manual.  
- Inspect the filter housing and gasket for proper seating to avoid leaks.  

Choosing the right oil filter ensures your Harley-Davidson touring bike stays protected and performs at its best. Stick with trusted OEM or high-quality aftermarket options, and you’ll keep your engine running strong for miles to come.
