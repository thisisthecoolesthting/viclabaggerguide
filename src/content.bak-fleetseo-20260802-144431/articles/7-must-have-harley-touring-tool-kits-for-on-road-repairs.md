## 7 Must-Have Harley Touring Tool Kits for On-Road Repairs  

When you're out on the open road with your Harley-Davidson touring bike, the last thing you want is to be stranded due to a minor mechanical issue. A well-stocked tool kit can mean the difference between a quick fix and a long wait for roadside assistance. Whether you ride a Road Glide, Street Glide, or Electra Glide, having the right tools on hand ensures you can handle common repairs and adjustments. Here are seven essential Harley touring tool kits designed for on-the-go fixes.  

---

### 1. **Harley-Davidson Factory Touring Tool Kit**  
Harley-Davidson’s own factory touring tool kit is a reliable choice, designed specifically for their bagger and touring models. It includes:  
- **Screwdrivers (flathead and Phillips)** for electrical and fastener adjustments.  
- **Allen wrenches** in common sizes for handlebar and fairing bolts.  
- **A combination wrench set** covering most Harley fastener sizes.  
- **A tire pressure gauge** for quick checks before long rides.  

This kit is compact enough to fit in a saddlebag and covers basic maintenance needs.  

**Best for:** Riders who want OEM-quality tools that match their bike’s requirements.  

---

### 2. **Cruztools Road Tech M8 Kit**  
The **Cruztools Road Tech M8** is a favorite among touring riders for its comprehensive selection. It features:  
- **A 3/8" drive ratchet** with SAE sockets (common for Harley fasteners).  
- **Torx and hex bits** for modern Harley models.  
- **A spark plug socket** for emergency plug changes.  
- **A durable nylon pouch** for easy storage.  

This kit is slightly bulkier but covers more complex repairs than basic OEM kits.  

**Best for:** Long-distance riders who want a balance of portability and functionality.  

---

### 3. **J&P Cycles Essential Touring Tool Roll**  
J&P Cycles offers a **tool roll** designed specifically for Harley touring bikes, including:  
- **T-handle Allen wrenches** for better torque on critical bolts.  
- **A compact adjustable wrench** for versatile use.  
- **A tire repair kit** with plugs and reamer for puncture fixes.  
- **A small LED flashlight** for low-light repairs.  

The roll-up design keeps tools organized and prevents rattling in luggage.  

**Best for:** Riders who prefer a compact, organized tool storage solution.  

---

### 4. **Motion Pro Trail Tech Kit**  
While originally designed for adventure bikes, the **Motion Pro Trail Tech Kit** is adaptable for Harley touring models. It includes:  
- **High-quality tire irons** for roadside tire changes.  
- **A compact chain tool** (useful for belt adjustments on some models).  
- **A multi-bit driver** with Torx, hex, and Phillips heads.  

This kit is rugged and built for tough conditions, making it ideal for riders who venture off the beaten path.  

**Best for:** Touring riders who also take their baggers on rough roads.  

---

### 5. **Biker’s Choice Premium Tool Kit**  
The **Biker’s Choice Premium Tool Kit** is a comprehensive set with:  
- **A breaker bar** for stubborn bolts.  
- **A torque wrench adapter** for critical fasteners like axle nuts.  
- **A wire crimper and stripper** for electrical repairs.  

This kit is heavier but covers nearly every roadside repair scenario.  

**Best for:** Mechanics-minded riders who want a full toolkit.  

---

### 6. **Tusk Motorcycle Tool Kit (Adventure Series)**  
The **Tusk Adventure Series** is another off-road-inspired kit that works well for touring bikes. It includes:  
- **A folding multi-tool** with pliers, wire cutters, and knife.  
- **Spoke wrenches** (useful for riders with wire-spoke wheels).  
- **A compact air pressure gauge** with deflator.  

The kit is lightweight and fits easily in a tour pack.  

**Best for:** Minimalists who want versatile, space-saving tools.  

---

### 7. **Custom Chrome Pro Touring Tool Set**  
The **Custom Chrome Pro Touring Tool Set** is a premium option with:  
- **Hardened steel wrenches** for durability.  
- **A magnetic pickup tool** for dropped fasteners.  
- **A fold-out tool mat** to keep parts organized during repairs.  

This kit is designed for riders who want professional-grade tools on the road.  

**Best for:** Touring enthusiasts who prioritize tool quality over portability.  

---

### **What to Look for in a Harley Touring Tool Kit**  
When choosing a tool kit for your Harley bagger or touring bike, consider:  
- **Compatibility:** Ensure the tools fit your bike’s fasteners (SAE sizes, Torx, etc.).  
- **Portability:** A compact kit that fits in a saddlebag is ideal.  
- **Durability:** Look for hardened steel or chrome-vanadium tools.  
- **Versatility:** Kits with multi-tools or adapters save space.  
- **Extras:** Tire repair kits, flashlights, and pressure gauges add value.  

A good tool kit should cover **basic adjustments (bolts, belts, tires)** and **emergency repairs (electrical, punctures)** without taking up too much space.  

---

### **Final Thoughts**  
No matter which Harley touring model you ride, having a dependable tool kit ensures you’re prepared for the unexpected. Whether you prefer a minimalist roll or a comprehensive set, the right tools can keep your ride smooth and stress-free. Invest in a quality kit, and you’ll never have to worry about being stranded by a minor mechanical issue again.
