## 5 Must-Have Harley Touring Accessories Under $100

When it comes to Harley-Davidson bagger and touring motorcycles, the right accessories can make all the difference in comfort, convenience, and style. Whether you’re planning a cross-country trip or just cruising around town, these five essential accessories are all under $100 and designed to enhance your riding experience.

---

## 1. **Handlebar Bag**

A handlebar bag is a compact yet highly functional accessory for touring riders. It’s perfect for storing small items like your phone, wallet, keys, or sunglasses, keeping them easily accessible while you ride. Many Harley riders opt for handlebar bags designed specifically for baggers, ensuring a seamless fit with the bike’s aesthetic.

**Features to Look For:**  
- Weather-resistant materials to protect your belongings from rain and dust.  
- Secure mounting systems that won’t interfere with your handlebar controls.  
- Reflective accents for added visibility during night rides.  

**Why It’s Worth It:** A handlebar bag eliminates the need to dig through your saddlebags for essentials, making it a practical addition for both short trips and long tours.

---

## 2. **USB Charging Port**

In today’s connected world, keeping your devices charged is crucial, especially on long rides. A USB charging port is an affordable accessory that ensures your phone, GPS, or other gadgets stay powered up. Many Harley riders install these ports directly on the handlebars or fairing for easy access.

**Features to Look For:**  
- Waterproof and dustproof design for durability.  
- High-output ports (2.1A or higher) for fast charging.  
- Plug-and-play installation for convenience.  

**Why It’s Worth It:** A USB charging port ensures you never run out of battery mid-ride, making it a must-have for riders who rely on navigation or communication devices.

---

## 3. **Windshield Bag**

A windshield bag is a versatile storage solution that attaches to the inside of your bike’s windshield. It’s ideal for storing items you want to keep within arm’s reach, such as maps, snacks, or a small first-aid kit. Many windshield bags are designed to complement the look of Harley touring models.

**Features to Look For:**  
- Expandable compartments to accommodate different items.  
- Transparent pockets for easy visibility of contents.  
- Durable construction to withstand vibrations and weather.  

**Why It’s Worth It:** A windshield bag maximizes storage space without cluttering your bike, making it a practical choice for organized riders.

---

## 4. **Adjustable Cup Holder**

Staying hydrated is essential on long rides, and an adjustable cup holder makes it easy to keep your favorite beverage close at hand. These holders are designed to attach to various parts of your bike, such as the handlebars or crash bars, and can accommodate a range of cup sizes.

**Features to Look For:**  
- Non-slip grips to keep your drink secure.  
- Adjustable mounts for flexible positioning.  
- Rust-resistant materials for longevity.  

**Why It’s Worth It:** An adjustable cup holder ensures you can stay hydrated without stopping, adding convenience to your touring experience.

---

## 5. **LED Accent Lighting**

Enhance your bike’s visibility and style with LED accent lighting. These lights are affordable, easy to install, and can be mounted on various parts of your Harley, such as the frame, engine, or saddlebags. LED lights not only improve safety by making your bike more visible at night but also add a custom touch to your ride.

**Features to Look For:**  
- Waterproof and shockproof construction.  
- Multiple color options to match your bike’s aesthetic.  
- Plug-and-play installation for hassle-free setup.  

**Why It’s Worth It:** LED accent lighting combines functionality and style, making your Harley stand out while improving nighttime safety.

---

## **What to Look for When Choosing Harley Touring Accessories**

When shopping for accessories for your Harley-Davidson touring bike, keep these factors in mind to ensure you get the best value for your money:  

1. **Compatibility:** Make sure the accessory is designed to fit your specific Harley model.  
2. **Durability:** Look for weatherproof and rugged materials that can withstand long rides and harsh conditions.  
3. **Ease of Installation:** Opt for accessories with straightforward installation processes, especially if you plan to install them yourself.  
4. **Functionality:** Choose accessories that enhance your riding experience, whether it’s improving storage, comfort, or safety.  
5. **Aesthetic Appeal:** Select accessories that complement your bike’s style, ensuring a cohesive look.  

By focusing on these criteria, you can find affordable accessories that deliver both performance and style, enhancing your Harley touring experience without breaking the bank.  

---

Investing in these five must-have accessories under $100 will not only elevate your ride but also make your Harley-Davidson bagger or touring motorcycle more functional and personalized. Whether you’re a seasoned rider or new to touring, these additions are sure to enhance your time on the road.
