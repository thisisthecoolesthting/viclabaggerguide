## Top 5 Harley Touring Security Systems to Protect Your Ride

When it comes to Harley-Davidson touring motorcycles, security is a top priority. These bikes are not just vehicles; they’re investments and sources of pride. Whether you’re parked at home or on the road, protecting your ride from theft or tampering is essential. Here are the top five security systems designed specifically for Harley touring bikes, each offering unique features to keep your motorcycle safe.

### 1. Harley-Davidson Security System (Factory Option)

The Harley-Davidson Security System is a factory-installed option available for most touring models. This system integrates seamlessly with your bike’s electronics, providing a reliable and tamper-resistant solution.

**Key Features:**
- **Passive Arming:** Automatically arms when the ignition is turned off and the bike is stationary.
- **Motion Sensor:** Detects movement or tilting of the motorcycle, triggering the alarm.
- **Key Fob Control:** Allows you to arm, disarm, and locate your bike with ease.
- **Immobilizer Feature:** Prevents the engine from starting without the correct key fob.

**Why It’s Great:** As a factory-installed system, it’s designed specifically for Harley touring bikes, ensuring compatibility and reliability.

### 2. Scorpio iON Motorcycle Alarm System

The Scorpio iON is a popular aftermarket alarm system known for its advanced features and compact design. It’s an excellent choice for riders who want robust security without compromising on aesthetics.

**Key Features:**
- **RFID Tag Disarming:** Allows you to disarm the system quickly using an RFID tag.
- **Smartphone Integration:** Connect the system to your phone via Bluetooth for remote monitoring and control.
- **Built-in GPS Tracking:** Provides real-time location tracking, even if your bike is moved.
- **Perimeter Sensor:** Detects movement around the bike and triggers the alarm.

**Why It’s Great:** The Scorpio iON combines cutting-edge technology with user-friendly features, making it a versatile security solution.

### 3. Dowco Guardian Alarm Disc Lock

For riders who prefer a simple yet effective security solution, the Dowco Guardian Alarm Disc Lock is a standout choice. This compact device combines a disc lock with a built-in alarm system.

**Key Features:**
- **Loud Alarm:** Emits a 120dB alarm when tampered with or moved.
- **Weatherproof Design:** Built to withstand harsh weather conditions.
- **Easy to Use:** Simply attach it to your bike’s brake disc for immediate protection.
- **Compact and Portable:** Fits easily in your saddlebag when not in use.

**Why It’s Great:** Its portability and ease of use make it ideal for riders who need quick, on-the-go security.

### 4. Kryptonite New York Fahgettaboudit Mini U-Lock

While not an alarm system, the Kryptonite New York Fahgettaboudit Mini U-Lock is one of the most secure locks available for motorcycles. It’s a favorite among Harley riders for its strength and durability.

**Key Features:**
- **18mm Hardened Steel:** Offers maximum resistance to cutting and prying.
- **Compact Design:** Fits easily through brake discs and other secure points on your bike.
- **Double Deadbolt Locking Mechanism:** Enhances security against tampering.
- **Anti-Theft Protection Offer:** Includes a reimbursement program for stolen bikes.

**Why It’s Great:** Its rugged construction and compact size make it a reliable choice for securing your Harley touring bike.

### 5. Monimoto 7 GPS Tracker

The Monimoto 7 is a standalone GPS tracker that provides peace of mind by allowing you to monitor your bike’s location at all times. It’s an excellent addition to your security setup, especially for long-distance touring.

**Key Features:**
- **Real-Time Tracking:** Provides instant notifications if your bike is moved.
- **Battery Powered:** No need to connect to your bike’s electrical system.
- **Geofencing:** Set virtual boundaries and receive alerts if your bike leaves the designated area.
- **Compact and Discreet:** Easily hidden on your bike for added security.

**Why It’s Great:** Its standalone design and advanced tracking features make it a valuable tool for protecting your Harley.

## What to Look for in a Harley Touring Security System

When choosing a security system for your Harley touring bike, consider the following factors:

1. **Compatibility:** Ensure the system is compatible with your specific Harley model.
2. **Ease of Use:** Look for systems that are easy to arm, disarm, and monitor.
3. **Features:** Consider the features you need, such as GPS tracking, motion sensors, or smartphone integration.
4. **Durability:** Choose systems that are weatherproof and built to withstand harsh conditions.
5. **Portability:** For on-the-go security, opt for compact and portable devices like disc locks or GPS trackers.

By selecting the right security system, you can enjoy peace of mind knowing your Harley touring bike is protected, whether at home or on the open road.
