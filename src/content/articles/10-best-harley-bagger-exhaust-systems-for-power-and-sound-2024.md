## 10 Best Harley Bagger Exhaust Systems for Power and Sound (2024)  

Upgrading your Harley-Davidson bagger’s exhaust system is one of the best ways to boost performance, improve sound, and enhance aesthetics. Whether you ride a Road Glide, Street Glide, or Electra Glide, the right exhaust can transform your touring experience. Below, we’ve compiled the top 10 exhaust systems for Harley baggers in 2024, focusing on power gains, sound quality, and build durability.  

### What to Look for in a Bagger Exhaust System  

Before diving into our picks, consider these key factors when choosing an exhaust:  

- **Material:** Stainless steel offers durability, while titanium is lightweight and corrosion-resistant.  
- **Design:** 2-into-1 systems maximize torque, while dual exhausts maintain classic Harley styling.  
- **Sound Level:** Some systems are street-legal (EPA-compliant), while others are louder for aggressive riders.  
- **Tuning Needs:** High-performance exhausts may require an ECU remap or fuel tuner.  
- **Installation:** Bolt-on systems are easier to install; others may need modifications.  

Now, let’s explore the best options available.  

---

## 1. **Vance & Hines Hi-Output 2-into-1 Exhaust**  
**Best for:** Torque-focused riders  

Vance & Hines’ Hi-Output system is a top choice for bagger owners seeking low-end power. Its 2-into-1 design optimizes exhaust flow, boosting torque for highway cruising. Made from high-grade stainless steel, it features a black ceramic finish for heat resistance. The deep, throaty rumble is aggressive yet refined, making it ideal for touring.  

## 2. **Screamin’ Eagle Street Cannon Slip-Ons**  
**Best for:** OEM+ sound and style  

Harley-Davidson’s own Screamin’ Eagle slip-ons offer a factory-backed upgrade with a louder, deeper tone than stock. These bolt-on mufflers retain the dual-exhaust look while improving airflow. They’re compatible with most bagger models and require no tuning, making them a hassle-free option.  

## 3. **Rinehart Racing 4" Slip-Ons**  
**Best for:** Deep, rumbling sound  

Rinehart’s 4" slip-ons deliver one of the richest exhaust notes in the bagger world. The internally baffled design enhances sound without excessive drone. Constructed from stainless steel with chrome or black finishes, they’re built to last and fit most Harley touring models.  

## 4. **Freedom Performance Radical Radius Exhaust**  
**Best for:** Bold looks and performance  

This dual-exhaust system combines old-school Harley styling with modern performance. The swept-back design complements bagger lines, while the mandrel-bent tubing improves exhaust flow. Available in black or chrome, it produces a deep, aggressive sound with noticeable mid-range power gains.  

## 5. **Bassani Road Rage 3" Exhaust**  
**Best for:** High-revving performance  

Bassani’s Road Rage system is engineered for riders who want top-end power. The 3" diameter tubing and optimized scavenging reduce backpressure, improving horsepower. The sound is crisp and loud, ideal for those who love an attention-grabbing exhaust note.  

## 6. **Cobra Neighbor Hater Slip-Ons**  
**Best for:** Maximum volume  

True to its name, the Cobra Neighbor Hater is one of the loudest slip-ons available. The straight-through design eliminates baffles for an unfiltered roar. While not for everyone, it’s perfect for riders who prioritize sound over subtlety. Made from stainless steel with a black finish.  

## 7. **Tab Performance Hammerhead 2-into-1**  
**Best for:** Balanced power and sound  

Tab’s Hammerhead system offers a great mix of torque and a deep, mellow tone. The 2-into-1 design enhances low-end grunt, while the hand-polished stainless steel construction ensures longevity. It’s a favorite among bagger riders who want performance without an overly aggressive sound.  

## 8. **D&D Fat Cat 2-into-2 Exhaust**  
**Best for:** Classic Harley styling  

The Fat Cat system retains the iconic dual-pipe look while improving performance. The stepped-header design enhances exhaust velocity, and the deep, bass-heavy sound is unmistakably Harley. Available in chrome or black ceramic.  

## 9. **Python Eliminator 2-into-1**  
**Best for:** Lightweight performance  

Python’s Eliminator system uses lightweight 409 stainless steel for reduced weight without sacrificing durability. The 2-into-1 configuration boosts mid-range power, and the slash-cut tip gives a sleek, modern look. The sound is deep but not overly loud, making it great for long rides.  

## 10. **SuperTrapp SuperMeg Slip-Ons**  
**Best for:** Tunable sound  

SuperTrapp’s SuperMeg mufflers feature adjustable baffles, allowing riders to fine-tune sound levels. The modular design means you can add or remove discs for more or less volume. Made from high-quality steel, they’re a versatile choice for riders who want customization.  

---

## Final Thoughts  

Choosing the right exhaust for your Harley bagger depends on your priorities—whether it’s raw power, a thunderous sound, or a sleek aesthetic. From Vance & Hines’ torque-focused 2-into-1 to Cobra’s ear-splitting Neighbor Hater, there’s an option for every rider. Always check compatibility with your specific model and consider professional tuning for optimal performance.  

Upgrade wisely, and enjoy the ride!
